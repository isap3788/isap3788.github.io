Questions, answered.

Is BackIn free for job seekers?

Yes. Profiles, matching, and your first coaching session are free. Employers pay to join the network.

How long does my break need to be?

Six months or more. Whether you were away for one year or fifteen, there's a track for you.

Do I have to explain why I took a break?

Never. You choose what to share. Most returners give a one-line answer, and we help you write it.

What kinds of jobs are on BackIn?

Full-time, part-time, and flexible roles in tech, healthcare, finance, operations, marketing, education, and more.
